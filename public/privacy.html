ISA Secured PDF — Privacy Policy

100% On-Device • Zero Cloud Storage

Last Updated: November 2026

At ISA Secured PDF (operated by PDF Engine Studio), user privacy and confidential data protection are fundamental architectural principles. Unlike traditional cloud-based document utilities, ISA Secured PDF is engineered as a 100% client-side, on-device application.

1. Zero Document Data Collection

We do NOT collect, view, store, upload, transmit, or process your PDF files, form data, signatures, redactions, or document text on external servers. All operations (including viewing, editing, permanent redaction, electronic signatures, splitting, merging, and compression) are executed strictly in local device memory (RAM) or sandboxed browser execution environments.

2. Local Data Storage & Caching

To provide seamless performance, offline capability, and save user interface preferences (such as dark/light theme, recent signature styles, or session recovery states), the application may utilize local browser storage mechanisms (chrome.storage.local, localStorage, or IndexedDB). This data resides exclusively on your device workstation and is never synced to external analytics or marketing servers. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser cache.

3. Browser Permissions & Purpose

The ISA Secured PDF Chrome Extension requests only the minimum permissions necessary for its single purpose:

  • contextMenus: Enables users to right-click PDF links or documents on the web and open them directly in the offline workspace.
  • host_permissions (<all_urls>): Allows users to load and process PDF files from any web URL into local browser memory without routing documents through third-party proxy servers.
  • scripting: Injects the local PDF document payload into the workspace tab for client-side editing.
  • storage: Retains offline settings and workspace preferences locally.
  • tabs: Opens the private PDF editor workspace in a new browser tab.

4. Payments & Subscriptions

Any optional payments or Pro license transactions are processed securely through authorized PCI-DSS compliant Merchant of Record providers. We do not store, process, or have access to your payment card details.

5. Third-Party Data Transfer

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transfer user document data to any third party under any circumstances.
